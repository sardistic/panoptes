1. The service

Panoptes ("we", "us") gives approved applicants an API key for the datasets listed at /v1/datasets. Some datasets are relayed from public sources ("open datasets"). Others are computed by Panoptes, for example fused events, incident-rate surges and radio activity summaries ("derived datasets"). Access is currently free and by invitation. We may add, change or remove datasets, parameters and limits at any time.

  • Do not share, sell or sublicense your key, or open several applications to multiply your limits.

3. Limits

Each key has a per-minute rate and a daily quota set by its tier, shown in every response's headers and at /v1/me. Do not try to get around them, for example by rotating keys or addresses, or by calling the map's own endpoints, which are reserved for the map. We may lower limits or suspend a key to protect the service, without notice when necessary.
